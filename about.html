Our Story

Freshly Prepared. Made Today. Since 2020.

Since 2020

Good Food.
Good Vibes.
Good People.

Urban Cafe was born from a simple idea: create a space where people could enjoy freshly prepared meals in a warm, welcoming environment. What started as a small dream in Lilongwe has grown into a beloved community hub where friends gather, families celebrate, and coffee lovers find their happy place.

Our menu changes daily, allowing our chefs to work with the freshest ingredients and create exciting seasonal dishes. While we love innovation, we never forget our roots — our Urban Legends menu features the dishes our customers have fallen in love with over the years.

Every cup of coffee is brewed with care. Every meal is made to order. Every guest is treated like family. That's the Urban Cafe promise.

— The Urban Cafe Team
